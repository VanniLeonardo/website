---
title: "Research"
description: "Research by Leonardo Vanni on dynamic 3D reconstruction, uncertainty estimation for 3D foundation models, and real-time vision systems."
---

My work sits where 3D vision meets geometry. I study how to reconstruct
dynamic and egocentric scenes as their structure changes, and how to estimate
when learned 3D geometry can be trusted. I am interested in models that report
calibrated confidence rather than a single point estimate.
