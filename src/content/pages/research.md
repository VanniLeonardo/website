title: "Research"
description: "Research by Leonardo Vanni: dynamic 3D reconstruction, geometry-grounded uncertainty for 3D foundation models, and real-time vision systems."
---

Most of my work sits where 3D vision meets geometry. I study how to
reconstruct dynamic and egocentric scenes as their structure changes, and how
to estimate when learned 3D geometry can be trusted — models that report
calibrated confidence instead of a bare point estimate.
